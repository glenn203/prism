The switch is now a native `input type="checkbox"` with the `switch` attribute where the browser supports it, labelled only by a `<label for>`, and still a 24px target. The default look is a still, flat track and thumb (the thumb jumps, the fill and colours change at once). Stretch, glint, press-grow, springs and drag play only inside a polish motion scope (`data-prism-polish` with `motion`, `all`, or an empty attribute). Public props, events and names are unchanged.
